# Dustreach

Original PeakRunner map (key `dustreach`), embedded in the binary from
`src/assets/maps/dustreach/`.

Built following [`map-pipeline.md`](map-pipeline.md), with the concept's
recommended answers taken without a separate review.

## The idea

A long desert crossing between two ruined strongholds:

- **Core:** long runs over open dunes; ruined sandstone strongholds with an
  exposed, sunken flag; forward and flank turret ruins; a monumental ruined
  arch on the central saddle; scattered ruin cover in the field.
- **New:** everything built. Each team's citadel stands on a raised terrace:
  a keep with twin pylon towers (spawn hall, two inventories, baffles
  behind both doors), an arcaded courtyard round a sunken flag court
  open to the sky, a watch tower with the sentry turret and sensor, a broken
  watch ruin carrying the plasma turret on the right flank, and a caravan dais
  landing pad with four deploy slots on the left flank. v2 adds a cistern
  level under the keep (the generator), a service tunnel to the watch tower,
  and a two-level storehouse (see below). The Sun Gate is a new
  triumphal arch whose 12 m opening the flag lane passes straight through.
  Sandstone, terracotta, bleached timber and blued iron materials, team
  banners with a sun motif, a clear desert sky and a synthesized wind loop.

## Choices

- **Name:** Dustreach (alternatives considered: Sunscar, Ochre Gate).
- **Ground bases, not floating:** the idea is ruins in the sand.
- **Flag distance 724 m** (1,300 m since the 2026-10-07 spread, below):
  longer than Cairnhold (then 532 m) to keep the
  long-crossing identity, without being too long for eight players.
- **The Sun Gate is high ground and cover only:** no neutral mechanic exists.
- **Slot:** it is intended for `dustreach`, embedded like the other
  original maps.

## Originality

Terrain comes from PeakRunner's own hash noise and an authored profile;
materials, sky and wind are generated by `dustreach_materials.py` and
`build-dustreach.py`.

## Layout

- Bases at world (1024, 130, 391) facing +Z (red) and (1024, 130, 1657) facing
  −Z (blue) since the 2026-10-07 spread (were z 679 and 1369), the structures 180° symmetric about the map centre; the ground is not mirrored. Its large layout is point-symmetric (the profile
along the flag axis and the side forms), and the middle round the centre
landmarks stays shared out to 180–290 m, and so does a corridor along the sewer (built for one half and turned for the other). Out toward the bases each half's hills and
gullies come from its own noise (`OTHER_HALF` 5045 in the terrain module),
like real land. Fairness is measured, not mirrored: the halves' slopes,
heights and the climb into each base match within tolerances
(`scripts/assets/fairness.py`), and a lone bot reaches each flag in about
the same time (`a_lone_bot_reaches_the_enemy_flag_on_every_map`). See
`docs/map-pipeline.md`, Terrain.
- Terrain along the flag axis: a dune ridge behind each citadel, its plateau,
  a basin in front, two transverse dune crests with a basin between, a dip,
  and the raised saddle at the Sun Gate (1024, 152, 1024). Diagonal crescent
  dunes cover the field; a wadi runs down each side and high dune banks stand
  outside the wadis (see "Spread and lanes" below).
- Twelve midfield ruins (broken walls, column pairs), in 180° pairs, spread
  along the longer field since 2026-10-07 and kept 80 m or more off the wadis.

## Build and test

From `src/`, with Python 3 and `scripts/requirements.txt` installed (numpy, Pillow):

```sh
python3 scripts/build-dustreach.py [OUTPUT] [--no-bake]
python3 scripts/test-dustreach.py
```

The default output is the embedded `assets/maps/dustreach`; the build refuses
to overwrite. Baked builds are byte-identical. 3,442 collision triangles per
citadel (budget 4,500, see `map-pipeline.md`), 1,248 for the gate and ruins,
19,200 render triangles, 34 terrain holes, 13 lightmap pages, pack about 15 MB.

The 24 tests cover floors and headroom, every ramp (open sky, closed
undersides), the engine's standing-support ray over every walkable region, no
accidental slopes, no overlapping floor plates, spawn validity, the sunken
flag open to the sky, transform invariance and the collision budget, unique
equipment IDs and circuits, deterministic distinct materials and sky, turret
sightlines into the hall, cistern, tunnel, tower room and both storehouse
floors, the underground and storehouse floors and headroom, the three new
ramps, exactly two ways into the generator room, hole cells on the grid and
covered with flat cut edges, the bridge route through the curtain gate,
terrain fairness (unmirrored halves, the sewer corridor shared) and slope bounds, ground under
every structure, gate symmetry and the open lane, bake determinism, the wind
loop and byte-identical rebuilds.

## v2: underground level and storehouse

Flag outside, generator inside behind exactly two entrances, tunnels linking
a team's own buildings, and a second building to split the fight. All
dimensions are local to a base, mirrored for blue.

- **Cistern (generator room):** 26.4 × 22.4 m under the keep, floor 2 m below
  the ground, 6 m tall, four columns, generator on a ringed basin. Exactly two
  ways in: a 29° stair from the keep hall (a railed opening in the hall floor)
  and one tunnel door (4.2 m wide, 4.5 m high) in its north wall. No spawns
  down there.
- **Service tunnel:** 6.4 m wide, 4.5 m headroom, lamp strips every ~5 m.
  It runs about 16 m north under the terrace, then 8 m west, into a room in
  the base of the enlarged watch tower. A ramp climbs to a landing and a door
  in the tower's back face. The tunnel is a defender and infiltrator route
  between the generator and the tower; capping stays on the surface.
- **Storehouse:** 20 × 20 m, two floors (0 and 5 m), roof at 10 m, right of
  the courtyard. A bridge at terrace level crosses from a new gate in the east
  curtain to its upper door. It has a third inventory station, four of the
  eight spawns (two per floor), a railed ramp between floors, crates, and
  baffles inside all three doors.
- **Spawns (v3):** none in the keep hall or the cistern. Four are in the
  storehouse, three in the courtyard arcades and one on the caravan dais; the
  nearest is 23 m in a straight line (over 30 m on foot) from the head of the
  stair down to the generator. `test_no_spawn_camps_the_generator_stair`
  holds this.
- **Tower back porch (v3):** a roofed porch outside the tower's back door, with
  a baffle wall 2.6 m out and a closed west end, so the only way in is from the
  east. `test_tower_back_door_porch_hides_the_exit_ramp` checks that no
  outside viewpoint (25 bearings × 3 distances × 3 heights) sees into the tower
  room, up its ramp or onto the landing.
- **Terrain holes:** 17 whole 8 m cells per base (`HOLES` in
  `dustreach_citadel.py`), all under the terrace or the tower. No lid is
  exposed, and the cut's edge keeps the flat site height.
- **Routes:** a Rust test sweeps the engine's full player body along
  hall → stair → cistern → tunnel → tower → back door → porch and courtyard → gate →
  bridge → storehouse → its ramp and doors, for both teams
  (`dustreach_underground_and_storehouse_routes_are_walkable`). Another checks
  both generators stand in cut cells, 2 m below the ground.

## Sewer: a culvert across the whole map

A cross-map tunnel (`scripts/assets/dustreach_sewer.py`),
built for counterplay: open mouths outside each citadel that defenders and turrets
can cover, and a slower crossing than the surface. World coordinates; blue is
the 180-degree rotation of red.

- **Culvert:** one 8 m cell wide wall to wall, 5.5 m clear inside, sandstone
  block walls, lamp strips and stone ribs every 16 m, a dark drainage channel.
  The red leg runs straight along x 1072-1080 from the red mouth (z 360 since
  the 2026-10-07 spread; was 648) to a
  cross hall under the Sun Gate (x 968-1080, z 1016-1032, 16 m deep); the blue
  leg leaves the hall's west end. It never passes under a citadel, cistern,
  tunnel or storehouse (12 m clearance, tested).
- **Floor:** 8.5 m under the dunes (headroom plus at least 3 m of cover) with a
  10° grade limit, 16° for the last 32 m into each mouth. Since the spread the
  656 m leg rolls under two dune crests and two basins (lowest floor about
  99.5 m), so its crests are rounded to at most a 2° bend per cell: a skier
  holding ski stays on the floor instead of lifting into the ceiling.
- **Mouths:** an open trench, x 1072-1080, z 296-360 (was 584-648), behind the
  red citadel's back-left corner, 16 m east of the watch tower. The portal is
  54 m from the red flag; the trench climbs about 16 m to the ground,
  steepest at the mouth (at most 22°), so its floor cuts into ground that falls
  toward the base there.
- **Midfield shafts:** an open 8 m drop shaft in each leg at the dune dip
  (red x 1072-1080, z 936-944), about 9 m deep, 99 m from the gate, 283 m from
  its own flag.
- **Flank shafts:** one per base, on the landing-pad side, at the end of a
  branch culvert east along z 392-400 (was 680-688; red shaft x 1152-1160). About 15 m
  deep; 134 m from its own flag, 96 m past the watch tower and 32 m past the
  pad. The other flank would need a branch across the citadel's front or under
  it, so there is only one.
- **Openings:** each shaft has a flush stone kerb, a grate leaf thrown open
  beside it, iron rungs (decorative; there are no ladders) and four short lit
  posts at its corners. Players drop in and jet out.
- **Terrain:** nothing is re-shaped. 228 cells are cut (156 before the
  spread); the 208 covered ones get
  a roof that copies the terrain exactly, so the dunes look and collide as
  before. Trench and shaft walls rise to the terrain edge on the grid lines.

Checks: Python `SewerTests` (symmetry, clearance from base rooms, grades and
cover, the roof is the terrain, body-band walk mouth to mouth and to both
flank shafts, walls up to the ground at every opening, no turret sees into
the covered culvert, no spawns in it, collision budget). Rust:
`dustreach_sewer_is_seamless_and_open_mouth_to_mouth`,
`dustreach_sewer_leg_skis_mouth_to_hall` (a skier entering at 30 m/s reaches
the hall at 15 m/s; at 16 m/s, at 8 m/s), `dustreach_sewer_shafts_and_mouths_jet_out`
(real jet movement out of every shaft and both mouths), and the ignored
`dustreach_surface_versus_sewer_crossing_time` probe. With one simple
ski-and-jet driver, mouth top to mouth top took 44.5 s over the dunes and
114.7 s through the sewer. A skilled player would close some of that gap, but
not the two right-angle turns and the climbs out.

Counts: 1,632 collision triangles for the sewer (272 of them the roof), 9,836
for the whole map; lightmap pages 13 → 20. The release timing probe moved
Dustreach's average tick from 108 to 120 µs (worst 253 → 327 µs). Walking
entries to the flag court (2), keep hall (3) and generator room (2) are
unchanged.

## Known gaps

- The sewer is untested by people: whether it becomes the default way in, how
  hard the mouths are to hold, and whether the drop shafts feel fair. Bots don't
  use it.
- Distant dunes now fade into a warm haze: `sky.fogColor` 0.80 0.69 0.52.
- Kit sensor, inventory and generator models are shared; turrets now use the
  kit's mount plus the client's runtime head.
- No real glow: lit surfaces are bright textures.
- Nobody has walked, skied or played it yet, including the new underground
  routes. Bots don't use the stair or the tunnel.
- Turtling: spawns no longer sit by the generator stair (v3), but the stair
  is still the only way from the keep to the generator, 30 m from the nearest
  spawn. Whether defenders can hold it too easily needs a playtest.

## Open doorways (2026-09-24)

Supersedes the keep-baffle and tower-porch notes above. The keep's front and
back doors and the watch tower's back door are open straight in; the porch is
gone. The storehouse keeps its baffles (it holds spawns). The sentry turret

## Survey, flag access, Capture & Hold and look pass (2026-09-24)

Supersedes the cistern, storehouse and spawn dimensions above (asset
`dustreach-citadel-v4`, gate `dustreach-gate-v2`). From a playability survey
and a visual audit.

- **Cistern:** floor lowered to 4 m below the ground: 8 m clear under the hall
  paving (was 6). The tunnel and tower room drop with it; the tower's exit ramp
  is 8 m long (26°). The stair still runs down the east wall, now 16.2 m long
  (29°), from a railed opening at x 9.3-13.2, z -5 to 2, and leaves 2 m of
  floor in front of its foot. Its side is open where it is under 1.1 m high.
  Still exactly two ways in (stair, tunnel door).
- **Storehouse:** one 10 m hall (floor 0, ceiling 10) instead of two 4 m floors.
  A 5 m mezzanine runs along the west wall at bridge level, entered through the
  6 m curtain gate; a landing and a 26° ramp join it to the hall floor. The
  ground door is 6 m wide under the mezzanine (4 m high), the north door 6 × 6 m.
  Baffles stay (a spawn room); the gate baffle runs the mezzanine's length so
  its spawns can't be seen from behind the citadel. Spawns: two on the hall
  floor, two on the mezzanine. Inventory at local (41.5, 17).
- **Gates and arcades:** keep doors 6 m wide × 5.5 m high (under the 6 m hall
  ceiling), curtain gate 6 × 6 m, arcade bays about 5.2 m wide × 5.4 m high
  (four columns a side, roof at 11.4 m). Courtyard spawns moved to the gaps
  between columns (z 17.6 and 11.2).
- **Flag court:** stays fully open to the sky. `route_checks.flag_routes`
  (airborne) finds 18 distinct routes to each flag (entries over the curtains,
  back breach, keep doors, gate and arcade roofs); the test asks for at least 12.
- **Capture & Hold:** three points, none active in CTF. The Sun Gate
  (1024, 152, 1024) is the centre point: its arch spans the flag lane, so it has
  eight render-only marker posts on the 12 m ring instead of a pylon. West Wadi
  (701, 950) and East Wadi (1347, 1098), on the wadis' midfield pans since
  2026-10-07 (were (880, 950) and (1168, 1098)), are `cnh_tower`s on levelled
  plateaus, mirrored through the centre and clear of the sewer.
- **Look:** procedural desert sky (deep blue zenith, sandy horizon, thin cloud,
  visible sun), warm sun colour, exposure 1.1, warm sky and sand-bounce ambient
  and a low haze. The sun keeps the baked direction. The old cubemap's streaks
  are gone because the cubemap is no longer drawn.
- **Visual fixes:** the terrace paving no longer runs under the curtains and
  back wall (its edges flickered along their outer faces); keep door wall and
  brazier caps, the drum bands, the tower-room slab and an obsolete turret
  shroud fixed. Z-fighting pairs 119 → 0 (except one pair inside the shared kit
  turret mount, which the kit owns) and no visible hovering edges; both are now
  tests (`DustreachPassTests`).
- **Counts:** 3,338 collision triangles per citadel, 432 for the two towers,
  9,988 total; 26,056 render triangles; 20 lightmap pages.
- **Still untested by people:** the taller rooms, the mezzanine, the new ring,
  and whether 18 routes make the flag too easy to take.

## Props and terrain shade

Dustreach scatters original props from `scripts/assets/props.py` (theme `dustreach`): sandstone boulders (solid), dry scrub, dune grass and bleached bones (render-only). The committed pack has 48 boulder, 700 scrub, 3200 tuft, 40 bones. That adds 35,103 render triangles and 1,728 solid ones (prop budget 2,500). Big props come in mirrored pairs, stay at least 80 m from each flag and 28 m off the ski lanes, and sink into the ground. The baked terrain shade map `shade.rg` (`scripts/assets/terrain_shade.py`) shadows the ground under structures, hulls and big props for the map's sun, plus terrain self-shadow and occlusion. About 1.7% of the tile is in shadow. See `docs/map-pipeline.md`.

**Cover and ground layer** (after the 2026-10-07 spread). 22 mirrored cover pieces (10 ruin wall, 4 rock cluster, 8 crates; 8 crouch-height, 14 full-height) stand beside the ski lanes and around the control points, blocking movement and shots. A crate stack's crates stand flush along its run since then: two crates used to leave a gap a crouching shot passed through. The grass layer adds 8,121 clumps in 98 meadow patches (80,006 render-only triangles). Scenery: 48 boulder, 1000 scrub, 40 bones. In total props add 111,902 render and 2,736 solid triangles (budget 4,000).

## Water

Two mirrored ponds, the **Oasis**, sit at the head of each wadi beside the citadels, at (787, 384) and its mirror (1261, 1664) since 2026-10-07: ellipses 28 × 22 m, turned 35°, carved
into the terrain by `scripts/assets/water_bodies.py` from the map definition's
`"water"` entry. The bed shelves from ankle depth at the shore through waist
depth to 3.2 m at the centre (about 1,980 m² each), a crest one grid step wide
holds the water in, and the banks take the ochre (wet sand) splat. They are real
`water_volumes`: they slow walkers, brake skiers hard, float swimmers and cost
extra jet energy (see `docs/map-pipeline.md`). They are 174 m from the nearest flag lane, on each citadel's flank, clear of the sewer, so they are a
choice beside the route, not a block across it; flags, spawns, capture rings
and holes are all well clear (checked by `water_checks.assert_ponds`). The
underwater tint is turquoise. No prop, cover piece or bot route is placed in
the water, and the legacy render-only plane is off.

## Modern outpost look (2026-10-05)

The citadels, the Sun Gate and the broken midfield walls look like a modern
desert outpost instead of sandstone ruins. Their layout, rooms and routes are
unchanged: collision is byte-identical, so the behavior traces didn't move.

- **Surfaces** (`dustreach_materials.py` v2). The kit slots are repainted:
  - walls: sand-colored composite plate, 2 × 1 m panels on a grid with dark
    seams, lit bevels, fasteners and a band every third course;
  - floors: dark deck plate;
  - ramps and treads: steel grating;
  - trim: gunmetal;
  - interiors: light 1 × 2 m panels;
  - banners: team panels with a chevron.

  The terrain layers and sky are unchanged.
- **Hard-surface detail** (`scripts/models/dustreach_models.py`, in
  `assets/models/structures/dustreach/model.glb`, shipped as the pack's
  `models.glb`). Three roots on one texture, 13,920 triangles:
  - `dr-citadel` on both bases;
  - `dr-gate` on the Sun Gate;
  - `dr-ruin-wall` on each of the six broken midfield walls.

  The detail is generated from the builders' own boxes
  (`scripts/assets/cladding.py`): the outdoor faces and open tops that
  nothing covers. On those it adds:
  - gunmetal copings, flush with every wall and merlon top and lipped over
    the faces that drop away, and trims round the exposed edges of slabs
    and roofs;
  - an amber light line under each coping;
  - fins up the long faces, kept clear of the team banners;
  - a skirt along each face's foot;
  - guards on the convex corners.

  Nothing stands in for the detail: the builder's walls stay drawn under
  it. The column ruins (prisms) have no detail yet.
- **Tests** (`test-dustreach.py`, `AuthoredModels`):
  - each model is placed where its structure stands;
  - copings stand at most 2 cm proud of the solid tops;
  - every model vertex lies within 0.35 m of the solid boxes.

Not done: the column ruins, the watch ruin's octagon and the arcade columns
have no detail of their own, the keep's sun emblem and the courtyard braziers
are the kit's, and nobody has played it yet.

## Spread and lanes (2026-10-07)

The bases were too close, so they now sit about twice as far apart, with
different outdoor routes, after the classic long maps. The citadels keep
their design; each whole base moved 288 m out and the field was laid out
again.

- **Distance:** citadels at world z 391 and 1657; flags 1,300 m apart (were
  724). Twice would have pushed the dune ridge behind each citadel into the
  map's edge hills.
- **Lanes** (each side the same, turned for the other team):
  - **Centre:** through the Sun Gate and over the two dune crests.
  - **Dune sea:** each side of the centre, the crescent dunes: rolling
    jumps and cover among the spread ruins.
  - **Wadis:** beyond the dune sea, a dry riverbed sunk 10–38 m into the
    dunes, winding down the length of the field. Its smooth floor falls
    gently from each citadel's flank (about 117 m) to a dry pan at midfield
    (98 m), where the wadi capture point stands, and climbs again to the far
    citadel: the sheltered fast road, 1.5–1.6 times the flag distance. The
    oases sit at the wadis' heads.
  - **Sewer:** underneath, lengthened with the citadels.
- **Checks:** `test_wadis_are_sunken_smooth_and_run_down_to_midfield` pins the
  wadis (sunk through the field, a smooth floor down to the pans, the capture
  points on them, no ruin in them); the terrain test's window, profile and
  flag distance follow the new field. The engine's sewer tests follow the
  moved cells; with the same simple driver, mouth top to mouth top now takes
  about 100 s over the dunes and 191 s through the sewer.
