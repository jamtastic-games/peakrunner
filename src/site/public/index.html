Know the ground.

Every map is built from scratch for PeakRunner. Servers choose the map and mode and rotate through them; the images are real captures from the game.

Seven ways to play

Same speed, different goals.

Capture the Flag

Take the enemy flag from their base and bring it home while yours is safe. Knock out their generator to shut down turrets and stations.

Capture & Hold

Stand on the towers to take them, then hold them. Every tower your team owns scores over time.

Team Deathmatch

First team to the frag limit. Each round rolls its own time of day, weather, wind and a twist, like heavies only.

Rabbit

One gold flag. Grab it and hold on while everyone else hunts you together: every 15 seconds with the flag is a point.

Team Rabbit

Fifty lives a side. The first death drops a flag, and while your team holds it, every kill takes two of theirs.

Football

No weapons. Pass, tackle and carry the ball into the end zone on the two stadium maps. Body checks hurt.

Duel

One on one. Challenge someone, or a bot, from the lobby tower and fight first to three rounds. Many duels run at once, and anyone can watch.

Make it yours

Your server. Your rules.

Tune the physics, invent a mode, write new rules or add a map. Mods are folders of plain files: host them, and players get them when they join, after they say yes.

Standalone game packages

The full game without the launcher. It runs straight away but does not update itself: download it again when a new release comes out.

These are playtest builds: they are not notarized or code-signed by Apple or Microsoft, so your system may ask you to confirm the first launch. Install help · checksums are listed on each download.
