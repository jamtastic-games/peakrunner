Privacy

  • No accounts, ads or tracking. This site sets no cookies and loads nothing from other sites, with one exception you choose: the gameplay montage on the home page. Until you press play it is only a picture from this site; pressing play loads the video from YouTube's privacy-enhanced player (youtube-nocookie.com), and YouTube's privacy policy applies to what it then does.
  • Playing online: a match server sees your IP address (to send you the game and to limit abuse), the player name you choose, your chat and your play. Your name, chat and play are shown to the other players in that match. Servers keep this in memory while you play; their logs record joins and departures by a number, not your name or address.
  • Player counts: the game makes a random number the first time it joins a server and keeps it in its settings file. When it joins, it sends a code made from that number and the server's address, so servers at different addresses can't tell they're seeing the same player. The official servers keep only a salted one-way hash of the code, by day and in total, to show on this site how many people have played; nothing ties it to your name, your IP address or your play, and only the two counts are published. Delete the game's settings file and you count as someone new.
  • Server list: when you browse servers, the game and this site ask our directory for the list. Community servers that announce themselves are listed with their public address.
  • Downloads and updates: downloading from this site, or the launcher checking for updates, sends ordinary web requests. This site does not keep access logs.
  • Cloudflare carries this site and the directory, and processes those requests under its own privacy policy. Gameplay goes directly to the match server.
  • Your computer: the game and launcher keep your settings on your own computer. They never store match passwords.
  • Servers run by other people follow their own practices.
